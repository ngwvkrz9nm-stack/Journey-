Pit Stops

An optional, earned break from tracking — never a penalty.

0Available
0Earned
0Used

Once taken, today is closed to tracking until midnight. You can take up to three days in a row.

Earning credits

  • 1 credit for every 18 Good Days (Goal + Progress)
  • 1 bonus for each run of 21 consecutive Goal Days
  • 1 one-time bonus at 90 recorded days

Credits never expire. Editing past days never removes or duplicates a credit.

Cooldown

After 1 Pit Stop day: 3 full days · after 2 in a row: 5 full days · after 3 in a row: 7 full days. Cooldown only limits Pit Stops — you can always check in.
