A Pit Stop is an optional break from tracking, not a requirement to eat differently. Once activated, today’s entry cannot be changed until the next day.

How to earn credits

One credit for every 14 cumulative Target Days, one bonus for each completed 21-day Target streak, and one one-time bonus at 90 recorded days. No duplicate rewards for the same milestone.

Cooldown rules

1 day: 3 full days off · 2 consecutive days: 5 full days off · 3 consecutive days: 7 full days off. Unused credits stay in your wallet.

Your journey settings
